1. Data We Collect

We collect the following information when you use NeuraChat:

  • Full name and email address on registration
  • Chat messages and AI responses during conversations
  • Login timestamps and session data

2. How Data Is Stored

All data is stored in a SQLite database on the Render server. Passwords are hashed using Python scrypt via hashlib and are never stored in plain text. The database is not publicly accessible.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your messages are processed by the Google Gemini API to generate responses. This processing is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained until you delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes all associated chats, messages, profile data, and membership records from our database.

5. Cookies

NeuraChat uses session cookies for authentication purposes only. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party analytics.
